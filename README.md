## Why a Specification-First Approach Matters

Canberra driveways are exposed to:

- Daily vehicle loads, including delivery and trade vehicles
- Reactive clay soils that swell when wet and shrink when dry
- Frost cycles that work into weak joints
- Tree roots lifting slabs and pavers
- Stormwater pooling on poorly graded surfaces

Specifications determine whether a driveway survives those conditions.

## Pre-Approval Validation Steps

1. Confirm the driveway type and finish.
2. Confirm excavation depth and base preparation in writing.
3. Confirm surface thickness and joint type.
4. Confirm removal of the old driveway and waste is included.
5. Check whether ACT Government approval is required.
6. Record the approved specification for future repairs.

Treat the approved driveway specification as the reference document: any later repair, extension or alteration should match the original depth, base and joint type.
